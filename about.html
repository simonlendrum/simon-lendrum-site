About Simon

Simon Lendrum spent decades at the top of Auckland's advertising industry, and is currently CEO of the Commercial Communications Council. A lifelong poker player and voracious reader of crime fiction, he'd been threatening to write a novel of his own for years. The 2020 lockdown finally gave him the excuse: he wrote The Slow Roll in under five months, and Upstart Press pulled it straight from the slush pile and published it immediately. It went on to win NZ Booklovers' Best Adult Fiction Book of 2023 and was a double finalist at the Ngaio Marsh Awards.
