About Simon

Simon Lendrum grew up in Newcastle-upon-Tyne, before heading south to study and work. In 2004 he and his family moved to Auckland, New Zealand, where he lives and works.

He's a keen poker player, scuba diver, and occasional (bad) golfer. He works in the advertising industry and writes O'Malley and Claire novels in his spare time.
